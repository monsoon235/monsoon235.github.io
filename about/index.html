About Me

2023-06-19 · Monsoon

I’m a 2nd-year PhD student of University at Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) since Aug 2024. Currently, my research interest is in Machine Learning Systems. I’m working with Prof. Minjia Zhang from Supercomputing System AI Lab (SSAIL). In the past I explored 3D vision (e.g., Feature Matching and NeRF).

I obtained my Master’s (2024) and Bachelor’s Degree (2021) from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).
